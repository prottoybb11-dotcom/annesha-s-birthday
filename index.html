THE FIRST INNINGS — WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

"Every story has a first innings, and ours quietly began in 2020. You know how, when you first meet someone, you usually just think it is that simple first impression? Somehow, with you, it felt a little different. I kept thinking about it, going over it in my head, wondering what exactly it was. Maybe I simply wasn't wise enough back then to understand what I was feeling. And how could I have been? I didn't even know you. But they say when an unfamiliar person somehow feels right to your heart, you don't really need time to know them—you just know. Sometimes, it takes less than a nanosecond. Maybe that was exactly what happened to me."

"And then, just when I had started trying to build a friendship with you, my luck decided to play its part—COVID came along. School closed on 17 March 2020. Everyone else might have been happy about the sudden holiday, but I really wasn't. After that, we barely had any contact at all. Looking back now, though, I feel like some of the best moments of my life were the ones I got to live in 2022. Even now, I sometimes wish I could go back to those days, because if I could, I would probably spend a little more time with you."

"Then, on 16 March 2022, we started talking again. But this time, something felt different. There was a strange little pull in our conversations, something I couldn't quite put into words—and honestly, I didn't mind it at all. I don't think I have forgotten a single moment after that. The whole story would probably take more pages than I have here, but those Truth or Dare moments? Those are the ones I remember far too well."

"And then came 19 February 2023. I was genuinely so happy that day—at least until I read the last three lines of the page you had given me. Until that moment, all I could think about was how happy I was that you had accepted me. Then those three lines told me that you were going to leave, and suddenly there was something else mixed into that happiness—a little sadness, because the person who had just accepted me was also going to be far away from me. But honestly, the happiness was still greater than the sadness. Because distance never really mattered if we were both still right there for each other. And perhaps what made me happiest of all was simply knowing that the person I had come to like so deeply had chosen to accept me too."

"And maybe that was the real beginning of our innings — not the day we first met, but the day we both chose to be in the same game."

— THE BIRTHDAY GAZETTE, SPECIAL EDITION

Editorial

PRESENT — THE GAME WE'RE STILL PLAYING

Honestly, I never thought long distance would feel this easy. I used to wonder about all the things that could go wrong, how difficult it might become, and whether we would really be able to make it work. But somehow, I don't have those worries anymore. If someone ever asked me about you, I could answer from the deepest part of my heart without even thinking twice: I'm the happiest person in the world to be in this relationship. The distance doesn't feel nearly as big as it looks on a map. I think I realised that a long time ago—not simply because I chose to believe it, but because you chose to stand beside me and made it feel possible. And I trust you enough to believe that you won't stop standing there. So, whatever the future brings, I know we'll face it together and eventually win this little battle against the distance.

And please remember one thing: I never want you to carry unnecessary worries about us. I haven't let them take over my mind until now, and I won't let them do so in the future either. I'll always want you to focus on your own life, your own dreams, and keep moving forward just the way you have been. We've made it this far by simply being there for each other, and I believe we'll keep doing exactly that. One day, you'll come into my life in a way that leaves no room for anyone to question us—no one left to say that this was impossible, no one left to doubt that we could make it. Because this innings isn't ending here; we're still playing, and I already know which side I'm betting on.

Ahh, I don't even know what I'm writing anymore—I'm just writing everything in one sitting, and somehow I almost forgot the most important thing: it's your birthday, and here I am writing instead of simply wishing you. So, Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️ Wherever life has you today, I hope you're happy, safe, and surrounded by everything that makes you smile. And once again, happy birthday to the person who became such a huge part of my life. I don't think I could ever properly thank you for that, because "thanks" is only six letters, and six little letters could never be enough to hold everything I feel for having you in my life. So I'll just keep saying it in every way I know—through every page, every memory, and every innings still waiting to be played. ❤️🏏

— The Birthday Gazette Editorial Board
